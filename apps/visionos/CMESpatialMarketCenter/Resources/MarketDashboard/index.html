Calculation assumptions and simulation limits

The paper ledger starts with $100,000 fictional USD. Cash ledger = starting balance + realized FIFO profit/loss. Equity = cash ledger + unrealized profit/loss. Margin is reserved, not deducted as a payment. Free demo equity = equity − illustrative margin.

P/L uses integer price ticks × fictional USD value per tick × signed contracts. Gross demo exposure uses synthetic price ticks × fictional tick value × absolute contracts. No FX conversion, commissions, financing, daily settlement, SPAN, maintenance margin, offsets, liquidation, or real execution is modeled.

The margin monitor warns at 70% utilization and flags exhausted equity at 100%. These thresholds are local demonstration rules, not financial risk guidance. Added exposure is rejected if illustrative margin exceeds paper equity. Risk-reducing closes remain possible with fresh synthetic quotes.

What this prototype is

Product names provide familiar context. Contract IDs, reference prices, history, increments, volume, depth, and trade prints are illustrative demo content, not official contract specifications or exchange records. Generated timestamps use this computer's clock in UTC.

Your demo watchlist stays in this app session. Quitting or reloading the desk resets it. There are no connected accounts, analytics, vendor SDKs, remote fonts, or market-data requests. Paper balances, positions, and fills stay in memory. Restarting a session preserves your watchlist.
