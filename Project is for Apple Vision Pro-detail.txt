Project is for Apple Vision Pro 
####
For **CME Group customers**—traders, brokers, clearing members and institutional clients—Apple Vision Pro could provide an immersive trading and market-analysis experience.

### Customer use ideas

* **Virtual Trading Desk:** Customers can open several large virtual screens for CME futures/options, order books, charts, news and positions.
* **3D Market Visualization:** View price movement, volume, volatility, open interest and market depth in an interactive 3D format.
* **Portfolio and Risk View:** See margin requirements, exposure, profit/loss and risk alerts across asset classes such as equity indexes, energy, agriculture, FX and interest rates.
* **Voice-assisted Market Search:** Ask questions such as, “Show today’s highest-volume energy contracts” or “Compare gold volatility with the previous week.”
* **Trading Simulation:** New customers can practise placing orders and managing risk using delayed or simulated data before entering the live market.
* **Product Education:** Interactive explanations of futures, options, spreads, expiry, settlement and margin calculations.
* **Virtual Client Support:** A CME specialist and customer could securely review the same charts, reports or platform workflow in a shared virtual room.
* **Market Event Experience:** Customers could follow major events—Fed announcements, crude-oil reports or agricultural releases—through live dashboards and alerts.

### Best initial product idea

A **“CME Spatial Market Center”** app with:

1. Live or delayed CME market data
2. Custom watchlists and alerts
3. Multiple virtual chart screens
4. Portfolio and margin-risk dashboard
5. Contract information and expiry calendar
6. Paper-trading mode
7. Voice and eye-controlled navigation

For safety, the first release should focus on **analysis, education and simulated trading**. Live order execution can be added later through approved brokers, with strong confirmation steps, MFA, entitlement checks and regulatory controls.
